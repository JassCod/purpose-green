# Purpose Green 🌱

Two browser games built with three.js. Both are static files with no build step, and three.js is vendored in `vendor/`.

## 🌍 World (`index.html`), an endless, realistic city builder
Explore a procedurally generated world with no edges, build a working city, and keep its citizens alive.

### Made in Blender
All 54 models (buildings, vehicles, people, trees, street furniture) are built by `tools/blender/build_assets.py` with Blender's Python API at real-world scale (1 tile = 10 m). They use PBR textures generated by `tools/blender/textures.py`: brick, roof tiles, siding, plaster, concrete, asphalt, bark, leaves, grass, rock, sand, snow, solar cells and water normals.
- **People**: male and female models with eyes, irises, brows, nose, lips, ears, hair, hands and shoes. Their jointed arms and legs swing as they walk, and each person's clothes are tinted per citizen (uniforms by job).
- **Vehicles**: sedan, hatchback, SUV, taxi, police car, ambulance, fire engine, city bus and delivery truck. They're built from real side profiles with glass, lights, grilles, bumpers, plates and mirrors, and the wheels spin with distance travelled.
- **Buildings**: 5 house styles, apartments, offices, shops, restaurants, bank, factory, farm (barn, silo, tractor), coal plant with cooling tower, solar farm, wind turbine (spinning rotor), water works, water tower, landfill, school, university with clock tower, hospital with helipad, police, fire station, construction yard with tower crane, and parks.

Rebuild them with `pip install bpy` and then:
```bash
python tools/blender/textures.py assets/textures
```
```bash
python tools/blender/build_assets.py
```
Preview the models in `tools/preview.html`.

### Graphics
Physically based sky (Rayleigh/Mie scattering) and image-based reflections, ACES tone mapping, soft shadows, bloom, SMAA, and ambient occlusion on Ultra. Terrain is texture-splatted, with 3D grass that sways in the wind, reflective water that freezes in frost, and drifting clouds. Distant trees switch to a lower level of detail. Quality presets (Low / High / Ultra) are in ⚙️.

### Laws of nature (`world/nature.js`)
- **Astronomy**: real solar position for latitude 42°N and the day of the year, so day length and sun height change with the seasons. Moon phases.
- **Climate**: seasonal and daily temperature cycles. Rain turns to snow below freezing, and heating/cooling demand follows the temperature.
- **Energy physics**: turbine power curve (cut-in 3 m/s, P ∝ v³, rated 12 m/s, cut-out 25 m/s). Solar output from sun elevation, air mass, clouds, heat and snow.
- **Water cycle**: rain and snowmelt recharge groundwater, heat evaporates it, and wells weaken in droughts. Lakes freeze in sustained frost.
- **Ecology**: crops grow only in the right temperature range. Saplings grow, and forests reseed cleared and burned land.
- **Wildfire**: dry lightning and heat waves ignite forests. Fire spreads downwind, rain and fire stations put it out, and it can reach buildings.
- **Physics** (`world/physics.js`, cannon-es, metres and seconds, g = 9.81 m/s²): a walking player with mass, jumping and swimming (buoyancy); a drivable car with raycast suspension, grip and braking; demolition rubble that tumbles; felled trees that topple; balls you can throw.

### A living city (`world/sim.js`)
- Power and water flow only along connected roads, with real supply and demand, so a power shortage can cut out the water pumps.
- About 70 occupations across every workplace: doctors, nurses, surgeons, teachers, professors, police officers, detectives, firefighters, chefs, waiters, bankers, software developers, lawyers, architects, engineers, welders, farmers, construction workers, crane operators, gardeners and more. Many jobs need a high-school education or a university degree.
- Citizens age, study, earn degrees, work, commute, get sick, have children, retire, die, and move away if unhappy.
- Emergency services respond to real events: fire engines drive to fires, ambulances to the sick, and police patrol.
- Budget, taxes, imports, pollution, crime, fires, seasons and storms.

### Transport
- **Railways**: draw track like roads (it can cross roads at level crossings). Stations on the same line link their districts, so people can commute to jobs along the railway. Trains (a locomotive and three carriages) accelerate out of stations, brake smoothly into platforms and stop to board.
- **Airports** (80 × 80 m): runway with markings and lights, terminal, control tower, hangar and windsock. Turboprops push back, taxi, take off, climb out, then return, land, roll out and park at the gate. Airliners cross the sky at cruising altitude.
- **Seaports**: container cranes over the quay. Cargo ships and ferries find real routes through rivers, lakes and bays and dock to unload, which makes imports 40% cheaper. Sailboats roam the water and heel with the wind. Ice stops ships.
- New jobs: train drivers, conductors, station masters, pilots, flight attendants, air-traffic controllers, aircraft mechanics, dock workers, ship captains, sailors and customs officers.

### Controls
Drag to pan, right-drag or `Q`/`E` to rotate, scroll to zoom, `WASD` to pan. `F` walks around on foot (`Space` jumps, click throws a ball). `C` drives a car (`W`/`S` for gas and brake, `A`/`D` to steer, `Space` for the handbrake, `R` flips it back). Also: `R` rotates a building, `B` bulldozes, `H` hides the UI, `Space` pauses.

**360° viewing**: the view pad above the minimap rotates (⟲ ⟳) and tilts (▲ ▼) the camera, the compass turns you back to north, 🔄 auto-orbits a full 360°, and 🛸 (or `G`) opens a free-flying drone camera (drag to look in any direction, `WASD` to fly, `E`/`Q` for up and down). On foot you can look straight up and down, and while driving you can drag to swing the camera all the way around the car.
